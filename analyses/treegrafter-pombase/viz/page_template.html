How much of TreeGrafter rests on PomBase evidence?

TreeGrafter (GO_REF:0000118) annotates UniProt proteins by placing them onto PANTHER family trees and copying GO terms from annotated ancestral nodes. Those ancestral annotations (IBDs) are built from experimental annotations at the leaves. This page traces each TreeGrafter annotation back up the tree to the IBD it came from, and asks whether PomBase evidence is behind it.

Method

Three records, joined through the tree

A TreeGrafter row names the PTN its protein was grafted onto, not the node the GO term came from. The term usually comes from an ancestor several steps up. GO-DB has no PANTHER tree, so I built one: 3,549,262 child→parent PTN edges from the 15,683 PANTHER 19 family trees, with AN labels mapped to PTN IDs through PANTHER's annotation file. Every one of the 1,290,658 graft PTNs cited by TreeGrafter is in these trees.

3 · IBA row in GOA

Read the evidence behind the IBD

Q9P6I7 dea2 IBA GO:0043103 PANTHER:PTN000150991|PomBase:SPBC1198.02|SGD:S000005085

IBA rows carry the IBD node plus the leaves with experimental evidence. Here: S. pombe dea2 and S. cerevisiae AAH1. PomBase contributed; it is shared with SGD.

Rules. A TreeGrafter annotation is PomBase-supported if the graft node or one of its ancestors carries an IBA for the same GO term whose with/from cites a PomBase: ID. It is PomBase-only if no IBA for that term on the path cites any other non-PANTHER source. Taking the nearest same-term ancestor rather than any ancestor changes the total by 12,537 annotations (21,378,799 vs 21,391,336).

Worked example 1 · PTHR43114 adenine deaminase

One PomBase-backed node feeds a whole clade

AN6 carries IBAs for adenine deaminase activity, adenine catabolism and hypoxanthine salvage, based on S. pombe dea2 (SPBC1198.02) and S. cerevisiae AAH1, plus UniProtKB evidence (A. nidulans, Pseudomonas, Streptomyces, and for the activity term Leishmania) for the first two. Hypoxanthine salvage rests on PomBase and SGD alone. Orange circles are graft nodes, sized by how many proteins TreeGrafter placed there. Select one to trace its path to the source.

The AN1 clade (Chloroflexus, Streptomyces SCO4644/SCO5662) sits outside AN6 and has no IBA for these terms, so grafts there get nothing from it and are not drawn.

Worked example 2 · PTHR28283 cAMP phosphodiesterase

TreeGrafter lags behind a refined PomBase-only IBD

The root node AN0 now carries four IBAs. Both phosphodiesterase activities cite PomBase, CGD and dictyBase (cAMP also SGD). Negative regulation of adenylate cyclase-activating glucose-activated G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway (GO:0110034) cites only PomBase:SPCC285.09c (cgs2). Negative regulation of cAMP/PKA signal transduction (GO:0141162) cites only CGD. Every graft in the family sits below the root, so the two phosphodiesterase terms reach all of them.

In the previous snapshot the PomBase-only IBD was the broader parent term GO:1902660, negative regulation of glucose mediated signaling pathway. PAINT has since refined it, but the TreeGrafter run still assigns the old parent term, and nothing in this build assigns GO:0110034 or GO:0141162 through TreeGrafter. For example, a Rhodoferax protein grafted onto AN4:

Q21RG5 Rfer_3939 involved_in GO:1902660 GO_REF:0000118 IEA PANTHER:PTN002001421 taxon:338969

2,350 TreeGrafter annotations use GO:1902660 and none of them now matches an IBA for the same term, so this analysis leaves them unresolved, not PomBase-supported. They still carry a fungal glucose-signalling process term onto bacterial proteins. It is worth checking whether the IBD should sit lower in the tree or the term should be excluded from bacteria with a taxon constraint.

Notes

What these numbers do and don't say

  • Contribution, not dependency. Most PomBase-supported IBDs also cite other groups. The PomBase-only figure (0.99M) is the part that would plausibly lose its experimental basis without PomBase.
  • Versions. PANTHER 19 trees are matched against this build, whose TreeGrafter rows are dated 2026-07-27. All graft PTNs were found in the trees. This build is smaller than the September 2025 one (675M rows, against 962M) and has no TreeGrafter annotations for viruses; distinct taxa reached fell from about 848k to 91k, so absolute numbers are not comparable between the two.
  • Not modelled. NOT annotations on intermediate nodes that would block inheritance, and UniProtKB IDs for pombe proteins cited instead of PomBase IDs (these would make the PomBase-only count slightly low).
  • Unresolved. 9.9% (16.5M) of TreeGrafter annotations have no same-term IBA on their path. For 3.0M of them an ancestor has an IBA to a more specific term, as in example 2 (452k of those cite PomBase). 1.27M have no IBA ancestor at all. The rest have IBA ancestors only for unrelated terms, which most likely means IBDs that were removed or changed after the TreeGrafter run.
  • Reproduce. make treegrafter-pombase in the go-db repository downloads the PANTHER trees, builds the PTN edge table, runs analyses/treegrafter-pombase/ptn_ancestry_contribution.sql and viz/export_viz_data.sql against db/goa_uniprot_all.ddb, and rebuilds this page.
